Rhythmic clatter of train wheels on tracks, steady and metallic, fading as the train slows. Inside the carriage, passengers shuffle and settle, soft friction of fabric against seats, a bag zipper closing with a sharp zip. A woman's voice, soft and weary, says, "Are we there yet?" A man replies, tone light, "Just a few more stops, don't worry." Outside, wind whistles through the window frame, a low, mournful hum. A child laughs, bright and sudden, followed by the rustle of a paper bag as someone pulls out a snack. The train slows further, metal screeching against the rails. A conductor's voice crackles over the intercom, "Next stop: Central Station." The announcement repeats, clearer. The doors hiss open, rush of cool air mixes with footsteps on the platform. A vendor calls out, "Fresh coffee, hot and strong!" The woman stands, coat rustling, and says, "Let's go." The man follows, footsteps echoing as they step onto the platform, train doors closing behind them with a heavy thud.
